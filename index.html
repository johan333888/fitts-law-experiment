Trembling-Finger Springboard

Scenario: you are an older adult with a mild hand tremor, selecting an app on a smartphone home screen. Your on-screen pointer trembles like a real fingertip — it will not land exactly where you aim.

Task: the moment a screen appears, one app turns green. Tap it as fast and accurately as possible. All other apps stay grey.

  • 5 icon sizes are tested, 5 correct taps per size (25 trials total).
  • Icon size changes automatically between blocks — the grid always fills the screen.
  • A CSV of your Movement Times is generated automatically at the end.
